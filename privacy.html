Visitor privacy

Updated 26 October 2026. This portfolio is operated by Akshay Kumar.

Optional visitor alerts

With your permission, a visit notification is sent to the owner's private Telegram chat. It contains the visited page, time in India, device category, operating system category and referring website hostname when available. URL query strings, precise location, names, email addresses, phone numbers and MAC addresses are not collected by this feature. We do not use fingerprinting or third-party advertising trackers.

Notifications are delivered through Telegram. Telegram processes message content under its privacy policy. They may remain in the owner's chat until deleted; this feature does not automatically delete delivered messages.

IP addresses and retention

Your public IP is necessarily processed by the web server and hosting/CDN provider. For this alert feature, a keyed hash of the connection address is used for abuse prevention; the raw IP is not sent to Telegram or stored in the feature's rate-limit file. Session hashes expire after 30 minutes and are cleared on subsequent traffic. Hosting access and security logs are managed separately by the hosting provider. This feature does not perform a location lookup.

Your choice

Analytics is off until you choose “Allow analytics.” Your choice is saved in this browser's local storage; a random session identifier prevents repeated notifications during the same browser session. Privacy settings on the portfolio let you withdraw permission. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control signals are respected. Blocking scripts or clearing storage can affect tracking.
