How your project moves forward

  1. Requirements: We discuss your business, intended customers, content and the tasks the website needs to handle.
  2. Scope and quotation: We agree the pages, features, revisions, milestones and support responsibilities before work starts.
  3. Build and review: You review the design and agreed functionality, including the mobile experience.
  4. Launch and handover: We test the website, deploy it and arrange account access, source handover and any agreed maintenance.

Questions before hiring a web developer

How much does a website cost?

The quotation depends on the design, number of pages, content, booking or dashboard features, integrations and ongoing support. Share your requirements for a project-specific estimate.

Do you work with Laravel and Django?

Yes. My project experience includes Laravel, Django, Flask, HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The choice depends on the features and hosting requirements.

Will my website work on mobile?

Responsive layouts and mobile review are part of the development process. We check the agreed pages and customer flows before launch.

Are hosting and maintenance included?

They can be included in the quotation with a defined plan, coverage period and update allowance. Renewals, third-party costs and additional features are agreed separately.
